# Marking system

Objective answers will be marked deterministically in trusted server-side code. Normalisation rules must be explicit and versioned. Extended answers use stored rubric points; keyword presence alone must not award marks where context or contradiction matters.

Correct rules are never included with pre-submission question data. Submission will lock the attempt, mark it transactionally, store rubric evidence and update mastery. Students then receive marks, their answer, the expected reasoning and targeted feedback. Optional AI assistance cannot be the sole source of final marks and is outside the free core path.

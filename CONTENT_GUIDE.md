# Content guide

All educational content must be original, accurate and written in British English. Do not reproduce copyrighted exam-board questions or another platform’s notes.

Each topic should have a short description, realistic study time, measurable learning objectives and a recommended next activity. Lessons should use concise headings, definitions, examples and common misconceptions. Questions need an explicit type, difficulty, mark value, explanation and testable answer rule or rubric.

Content progresses through `draft`, `published` and `archived`. A course shell or representative sample must never be described as a complete specification. Accessibility text is required for diagrams, and meaning must not depend on colour alone.
